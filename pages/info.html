sienna broglie

Sienna Broglie is a Montana based artist who began painting in 2012. She earned her BFA from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago in 2020. Broglie has exhibited at the Zhou B Art Center in Chicago, the International Museum of Surgical Science in Chicago, and Radius Gallery in Missoula, among others. She received the Naomi Winston Art Award and a Scholastic Gold Key in 2016. Broglie co-founded The Making Place, a nonprofit makerspace in Columbia Falls, Montana, serving as co-chair from 2025 to 2026.
After a six-year hiatus spent gaining life experience and inspiration, Broglie resumed her painting practice in 2026. As Werner Herzog once said, “Roll up your sleeves and work [...] [art] must have experience of life at its foundation.” Through years of backcountry work in the mountains and maritime work along the eastern and western seaboards, Broglie developed artistic ideas through sketchbooks, screenprints, and paintings. With her return to a dedicated art practice, she brings insights and knowledge grounded in her experiences of the natural world. Broglie holds a deep reverence for scientific discovery and seeks to highlight the continual expansion of human understanding of the world around us.
